# Octopye Google Organic Reach Checklist

After publishing:
- Verify octopye.com in Google Search Console.
- Submit https://octopye.com/sitemap.xml.
- Inspect and request indexing for the home page, services page, websites page, custom software page, automation page, pricing page, work page and contact page.
- Do not add Google Business Profile links, LocalBusiness claims or GBP wording until a real profile exists and has been verified.
- Do not add social profile links unless real current social profiles exist.
- Current external profile references are the Google Play developer profile and Chrome Stats publisher profile.
- Add real reviews, real project screenshots and natural client or partner mentions over time.
- Improve pages that earn impressions but weak clicks.
