Organic Google reach

Stop relying on paid clicks to make web design enquiries happen.

If Google reach only works when ads are running, the organic system is not trusted enough yet. Octopye needs stronger proof, clearer search intent, crawlable internal links and more off-site signals that show the business is real.

Estimate the website planAsk for the reach plan

What is going wrong

Paid traffic is masking four organic gaps.

This is not fixed by stuffing keywords into pages. Google has to discover the site, understand the offer, trust the business and see enough evidence that other people know about it.

Not enough trust

New visitors need proof: project pages, real screenshots, client context, reviews and visible contact details.

Not enough intent depth

One generic SEO page will not carry searches for web design, app design, industry websites, redesigns and static sites.

Not enough prominence

Google local visibility is helped by reviews, mentions, backlinks and a complete Business Profile where the business is eligible.

Not enough measurement

Search Console, analytics events and enquiry tracking need to show which pages get impressions, clicks and forms.

Day 0 Google setup

The launch checklist before waiting for rankings.

These actions do not buy rank. They make sure Google can find, inspect and understand the pages that already exist.

Search Console actions

  1. Verify octopye.com in Google Search Console.
  2. Submit https://octopye.com/sitemap.xml.
  3. Inspect the home page, web design page, SEO page, pricing page, tools page and five strongest industry pages.
  4. Request indexing after publishing the updated static site.
  5. Check queries weekly and improve pages that get impressions but low clicks.

Google Business Profile actions

  1. Use the exact public business name consistently: Octopye Digital Designs.
  2. Select the closest legitimate categories, such as website designer or web design company if available.
  3. Add services for website design, app design, SEO, hosting and website care.
  4. Add project images, screenshots, logo assets and regular posts.
  5. Ask every real client for a review and reply to every review professionally.

No spammy local pages

Local reach for a web design business should not mean fake area pages.

Octopye can work remotely, so the stronger organic route is service and industry intent. Local reach should come from a complete profile, reviews, case studies, local partnerships and consistent business mentions.

Relevance

Make the offer impossible to misunderstand.

Website design is the primary service. App design, SEO, hosting and care support that main offer.

Distance

Use the real business location or service-area setup.

Do not invent city landing pages. Use the actual Google Business Profile details once confirmed.

Prominence

Earn signs that the business exists outside its own website.

Reviews, client backlinks, directory profiles, partner mentions and project references build trust over time.

Conversion

Turn every organic visit into a clear next step.

Use the estimator, pricing pages, case studies and booking form as the main organic funnel.

90-day organic system

The work that can create reach without paid ads.

Organic search takes time because Google has to crawl, index, compare and test the pages. The quickest practical route is steady proof-led publishing and internal linking.

Weeks 1-2: fix discovery

  • Submit sitemap and inspect URLs
  • Link the organic reach page from SEO, services and footer
  • Make every project page a stronger case study
  • Check titles and descriptions for click intent

Weeks 3-6: build proof

  • Publish one real project note each week
  • Add client context and screenshots where allowed
  • Ask for reviews and website credit links
  • Create service pages from real buyer questions

Weeks 7-12: compound

  • Refresh pages getting impressions
  • Improve internal links to pages that almost rank
  • Create comparison and pricing content
  • Use Search Console data to choose the next page

Priority organic pages

The pages that should get most internal links.

HTML, CSS and JS web design

The main money page. It should receive internal links from services, pricing, estimator, industries and project pages.

Small business websites

The practical buyer page for clients who know they need a new website but not a large agency project.

Affordable website pricing

Pricing can convert organic users because it removes uncertainty and filters low-fit enquiries early.

Instant project estimator

The free estimator can earn attention because it gives useful price guidance before the enquiry.

Project case studies

Proof pages help both search engines and buyers understand what Octopye actually creates.

Industry website pages

Industry pages should answer specific buying questions rather than behave like copied location pages.
