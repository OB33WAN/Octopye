Packages and pricing

Packages built around launch scope and ongoing support

These packages separate one-off build investment from ongoing monthly support, so it is easier to understand what belongs to the launch itself and what sits alongside it as a retainer.

Included as standard

Hosting, security, and domain protection are included

Every build package includes the infrastructure and protection that many businesses end up buying separately. The aim is a clearer total cost and a cleaner launch process.

Cloudflare protection and management

Your website and domain are onboarded to Cloudflare for managed DNS, faster delivery, and protection against common availability issues from day one.

SSL certificate — included

Full HTTPS is provisioned, installed, and renewed without separate billing, which removes one more layer of routine admin and renewal risk.

DKIM, DMARC, and SPF records

DKIM, DMARC, and SPF records are configured as part of the build so the domain is better protected against spoofing and outbound email is more likely to land properly.

Domain security and hosting

Hosting setup and domain-security handling are included in the build fee, reducing the need for multiple providers before the site is live.
