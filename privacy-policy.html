Privacy policy

How Octopye handles enquiry information and privacy for website, software and support conversations.

Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, Octopye may receive your name, company, email address, optional phone number and the project details you choose to provide.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to your enquiry, understand the project and provide relevant follow-up. Do not send passwords, credentials or sensitive customer records through the public form.

Analytics

The site is designed to support privacy-conscious analytics and conversion tracking. Any analytics should be configured to avoid unnecessary personal data collection.

Retention

Enquiry information should be kept only for as long as needed to respond, manage the project conversation and meet reasonable business requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, email designs@octopye.com.
