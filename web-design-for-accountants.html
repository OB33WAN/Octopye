Buyer questions

Questions visitors need answered before they enquire.

Should accountants have client-type pages?

Yes when the firm serves distinct groups. Client-type pages help visitors self-select and make enquiries more qualified.

What should the form ask?

Ask for business type, service needed, current system, urgency and whether they are switching accountants.

Can a small accountancy website rank organically?

It can earn visibility when service pages are specific, technically clean, internally linked and genuinely useful to the target client.
