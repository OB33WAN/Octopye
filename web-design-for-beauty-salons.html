Buyer questions

Questions visitors need answered before they enquire.

Should a salon website link to an external booking system?

Yes, if that is how appointments are managed. The website should make that booking route impossible to miss.

Are treatment prices important?

Usually yes. Clear pricing or starting prices help visitors decide faster and reduce hesitant messages.

What content helps salon SEO?

Specific treatment pages, aftercare advice, new-client guidance, gallery proof and clear booking copy.
