Buyer questions

Questions visitors need answered before they enquire.

Do electricians need separate domestic and commercial pages?

Yes when both are real services. Those buyers have different risks, language and proof needs.

What trust proof should be visible?

Qualifications, accreditations, insurance, safety process, reviews and clear contact routes.

Should certificates be in the navigation?

If certificates are a lead source, yes. They are high-intent searches and should not be buried in a generic services list.
