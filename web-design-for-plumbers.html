Buyer intent map

Plumbing sites need two routes: urgent help and planned work.

Emergency visitors want a phone number and reassurance. Planned-work visitors want services, proof and a sensible quote route.

Emergency leak or callout

Can you help now and where do I press?

Make the call CTA sticky on mobile and keep emergency copy short.

Boiler or heating quote

Evidence you handle heating work professionally.

Separate boiler, heating and plumbing services with trust proof beside each CTA.

Bathroom or install research

A reason to choose you over a cheaper quote.

Show project images, warranties, process steps and the quality of finish.

Local trust check

Proof that real customers recommend you.

Place reviews, credentials and service area cues near the form and header CTA.

Buyer questions

Questions visitors need answered before they enquire.

Should emergency plumbing have a separate page?

Yes. Emergency intent behaves differently from planned quote intent, so the page should prioritise speed, phone contact and reassurance.

Can a static site work for a plumber?

Yes. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript is fast, reliable and ideal for service pages, quote forms and local SEO content.

What should the enquiry form ask?

Ask for job type, postcode, urgency, preferred contact method and optional photos. Keep it short on mobile.
